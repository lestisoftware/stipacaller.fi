Questions

How much does it cost?

The first 7 days are free from when your first relative joins. After that one subscription, €4.99 a month, covers you and up to 5 family members. Relatives you invite don't pay.

Does Grandma need a phone number or an email?

No. You send her an invite link; she installs the app, taps the link and is in. Everyone picks a username, no phone number is ever asked.

Can you read our messages?

No. Messages, photos and calls are end-to-end encrypted on your phones. Our servers in Finland only pass along data they can't open.

Which phones does it work on?

iPhone and Android, in English, Russian, Ukrainian, German, Finnish, Spanish, Hebrew and Arabic.

Who makes StipaCaller?

Lesti Software Oy, a small software company in Oulu, Finland. Write to us any time: lev@lestisoftware.fi.
